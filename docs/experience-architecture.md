# LUME & FRAME
## Experience Architecture

## Philosophy

LUME & FRAME is not designed as a gallery.

It is designed as an experience.

Visitors do not simply view images.

They enter a world of light, form and silence.

---

# The Entrance

The first impression is a quiet moment.

A visitor arrives.

The screen becomes a doorway.

The first feeling:

"Something meaningful is waiting."

---

# Visitor Journey

## 01. The Entrance

Purpose:
Create curiosity and emotional connection.

Experience:
A slow introduction through light, movement and atmosphere.

---

## 02. The Gallery

Purpose:
Show selected visual stories.

Collections:

- Photography
- Jewellery
- Portraits
- Commercial Works
- Films

---

## 03. The Experience Museum

Purpose:
Share what Zack sees and feels.

Formats:

- Photography journals
- Short reels
- Visual essays
- Behind the scenes

---

## 04. The Studio

Purpose:
Introduce the creative philosophy.

Includes:

- About LUME & FRAME
- Creative direction
- Services
- Collaboration

---

## 05. The Contact Room

Purpose:
Create a warm invitation.

Not just:

"Contact me"

But:

"Let's create something meaningful."
